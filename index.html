Week 2 & 3: 13th April to 24th April

Reality hit as I started working on my first monthly report, the Loan in Arrears report. I had to work with a massive chunk of raw data that my senior extracted from SAS programming into Excel. From there, it was an endless cycle of data cleaning and preprocessing, dealing with a crazy amount of rounding and balancing between PBB and PIBB for Hire Purchase (HP) and Impaired Loan (IL) accounts. The following week, I moved on to a new beast called the Daily Recovery report. It involved more data cleaning, heavy rounding, and another massive dose of VLOOKUPs to transfer everything into a ready format. Watching the recovery progress slowly accumulate day by day was oddly satisfying, though.

Week 4 to 9: 27th April to 5th June

The Automation Awakening

Decided to take things into my own hands and started exploring Excel VBA macros on my own just to see what kind of magic they could do. I successfully automated the tedious data transfer process for the Daily Recovery report, then immediately built a complex 3D balancing rounding macro for the Make Model report. Jumped straight into coding dynamic rounding adjustments and automatic VLOOKUP mapping for the Loan in Arrears templates based on filename prefixes. Ended the month handling the Overall Delinquency report, compiling and structuring all this data to make it much easier for the team to do trend analysis.

Week 10 to 12: 8th June to 26th June

The Data Cleansing Era

Spent these weeks solving major database extraction bugs for the IT division. First, I built a "Phone Number Formatter" macro to detect and fix phone numbers where the starting '0' was cut off by adding '60' or '65' prefixes back in. Then, I developed a "Hirer/Guarantor Separator" to filter out scattered duplicate data across three different legacy systems, saving the bank serious money on SMS blasting costs. Finally, I built a post-separation formatter to fix a bug where 12-digit phone numbers were spilling over and corrupting the email column in the database.

Week 13: 29th June to 3rd July

Worked on a new weekly workflow macro called the "Multi-Category Master to Target Sorter" which processed five different categories from a master file and separated them into their corresponding target files based on eight different CACs. In the middle of all this, I bumped into a massive PBB fire drill. The alarm went off in the evening, and we had to follow procedure, slowly climbing down 22 flights of stairs to head to the open parking lot.

Week 14 to 17: 6th July to 31st July

Graduated to handling high-value accounts. I took on the HP_1mil and HP/FSF_3mil reports, zooming in on accounts with outstanding balances above 1 million and 3 million respectively. We had to check their B-score (Behavioral) to see the possibility of them turning into Impaired Loans in the future. I also built the "Base Listing Multi-Category Sorter" macro to track how many new accounts transitioned into newly Close Attention (CA) or Impaired Loan (IL) status. Ended July managing the Restructure & Reschedule (R&R) report, separating them out with different code numbers and recalculating the overall delinquency ratio since it changes when an account transitions into R&R.

Week 18 to 23: 3rd August to 11th September

Advanced Forecasting & Analysis

A heavy analytical phase. Assisted with the data transformation for Expected Credit Loss (ECL) data and helped my seniors compile the draft 2026 yearly report of Hire Purchase to send to BNM. Coded a brand new interactive project called "IL Ratio Sensitivity & Scenario Analysis," applying a Userform in Excel to forecast the future IL ratio if the impaired loan accounts were to increase. Handled the CA Breakdown, Margin Earnterm, and Luxury Car reports. By week 23, I brought out the advanced Excel functions—using UNIQUE, LET, and Power Query to completely optimize workflows into one massive unified table. The highlight was definitely joining the massive 60th anniversary Public Bank event at the HQ!

SSTF

Financial Rounding & Balancing

Project 1: Sudoku Rounding Alignment

Solved a 2D rounding mismatch problem across financial sheets by building a "Plan-then-Apply" VBA script. Evaluated horizontal and vertical constraints across 9 adjustable columns and 3 groups (NEW, RECON, Secondhand), ensuring vertical and horizontal zero-variance without cascading Excel recalculations.

Project 10: Rounding with Spacing & Correction

A 3-step macro automating spacing, dynamic rounding formulas, and autonomous correction. The script scans raw data for remainders closest to the 0.5 boundary and applies ±1 adjustments automatically to resolve floating rounding differences without manual intervention.

Data Cleansing Architecture

Project 2 & 4: Phone Formatting Suite

Fixed major IT extraction bugs. Scanned raw matrices to rebuild dropped leading zeros, correctly prefix 60/65 country codes, and salvaged 12-digit phone numbers that IT systems were incorrectly truncating and spilling into email columns.

Project 3 & 8: Hirer/Guarantor Separator

Processed 300,000+ rows of scattered loan data via Dictionary and Collection objects. Separated primary hirers from guarantors based on a multi-tier address match, significantly reducing SMS blasting costs by eliminating duplicate numbers.

Project 11: Postcode & State Extractor

Engineered a two-tier text-mining tool to extract standard 5-digit postcodes and state names from messy, free-text Malaysian addresses, applying dictionary mapping and char-by-char extraction to fix glued strings (e.g. "Jaya47100").

Automated Report Routing

Project 5, 7, & 9: Master-to-Target Sorters

Replaced repetitive copy-pasting across 48 sheets. Built macros to auto-detect master file categories, sort data dynamically, insert required formula columns, and route data securely into pre-formatted target workbooks across 8 branches instantly.

Project 6: Dynamic Format Painter

Built a standalone script to automatically detect target files from open workbooks, find the dynamic last row and column of new data, and extend formatting rules (borders, alignments, number formats) without touching values.

Scenario Analysis

Project 10: IL Ratio Sensitivity Forecast

Created an interactive dashboard using UserForms that takes current Impaired Loans (IL), projected portfolio growth, and write-offs to reverse-engineer exactly how much IL can grow/shrink next month while still landing on management's target ratios.
